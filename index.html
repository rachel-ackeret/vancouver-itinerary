Before you go

    Worth remembering

    • Winter tires are the law on Highway 99 from 1 October.Tires marked M+S or with the mountain-snowflake symbol. Rental desks at YVR don't assign them by default — say it out loud when you book.
    • The gondola is the only weather-dependent thing here.Weekdays 9–4, weekends 9–5 through 30 October. Take whichever day looks clearest and buy the ticket that morning.
    • Dark by six.Anything outdoors wants to happen before four. The spa runs to 10pm, which makes it a way to end a day rather than fill one.
    • Whistler's lifts are shut.Peak 2 Peak closed 7 September, the Village Gondola 21 September, ski season opens around 20 November. Nothing here depends on them.
    • Expect rain.That's the trade for empty trails and shoulder rates. Waterfalls run harder and the Nordic baths are better for it.
